## About Me

I am a Mobile and Web Application Developer with strong expertise in React Native, MERN Stack, Next.js, and Nest.js. With 3+ years of experience and 30+ projects delivered, including dating apps, live streaming platforms, fintech solutions, and real-time tracking systems, I build fast, scalable, secure, and user-friendly applications. I specialize in creating strong application logic and smooth functionality using tools like Tailwind CSS, Redux, Socket.io, Firebase, Supabase, Agora, Zego Cloud, Zoom Integration, and REST APIs. I work with a problem-solving mindset, always focusing on client goals and delivering reliable, high-quality solutions.

- Client requirements and quality are always at the center of my work

## How I Work

-  **Client-First Mindset** — I keep client requirements and planning at the center of every project, ensuring the final product matches the vision exactly.
-  **Clean Architecture** — I write structured, maintainable code with clear separation of concerns, making projects easy to scale and hand off.
-  **Problem-Solving Approach** — I focus on understanding the root of a problem before writing a single line of code, which saves time and avoids rework.
-  **Consistent Communication** — I provide regular updates throughout the project lifecycle so clients always know where things stand.
